Hotmail guide

Hotmail full? How to free up space in your Hotmail, Live or MSN email

If Hotmail, Live.com or MSN says your mailbox is full, you are already on Outlook.com under the same address. Free space with the steps below, wait for the storage meter to catch up, then ask people to resend anything that bounced while you were over the limit.

For a deeper Outlook.com walkthrough, see Outlook storage full? Free up space safely. If send and receive already stopped, see Outlook can't send or receive because storage is full. If you already deleted a lot and the warning will not clear, see why Outlook still says full after deleting emails.

1. Hotmail, Live and MSN are Outlook.com now

Microsoft folded Hotmail, Live.com and MSN into Outlook.com years ago. Your old address still works; you just open it at outlook.com. The storage limits Microsoft publishes for Outlook.com apply to those addresses too:

  • Email storage — free accounts get 15 GB for mail. Paid Microsoft 365 subscribers get 100 GB of mailbox storage (trials do not get that extra space).
  • Microsoft cloud storage — free Microsoft accounts include 5 GB that OneDrive, Outlook.com attachments and Microsoft 365 apps share. If that shared pool is full, Hotmail can refuse send and receive even when the mailbox meter still looks open.

Go over either limit and Microsoft stops both sending and receiving. Mail that arrives while you are over quota is returned to the sender; it does not wait in your inbox.

2. Empty Deleted Items and Junk first

On a Hotmail, Live or MSN account, deleted mail still eats your quota while it sits in Deleted Items. Spam in Junk Email counts the same way. Clearing both folders is usually the quickest win.

  1. Log in at outlook.com using the Hotmail, Live or MSN address you have always used.
  2. Go to Deleted Items and pull back anything you still need into your Inbox.
  3. Choose Empty folder for Deleted Items.
  4. Switch to Junk Email, glance through it, then choose Empty folder there too.

3. Sort by size and clear big attachments

Old Hotmail inboxes often hold years of photo albums, scanned paperwork and video clips. Those few heavy messages usually reclaim more space than wiping short newsletters.

  1. Pick a busy folder — Inbox or Sent Items works well — while signed in at outlook.com.
  2. Above the list, open Filter > Sort by > Size so the heaviest messages rise to the top.
  3. Want only messages with attachments? Open Filter again and choose Has files.
  4. Download keepers you still need, remove the bulky messages you do not, then run Empty folder on Deleted Items once more.

4. Clear old mail on the Storage page

If your Live or MSN folders have years of backlog, the built-in Storage tools can clear whole age ranges without scrolling message by message.

  1. At outlook.com, open the gear (Settings) in the upper right.
  2. Open Account, then open Storage.
  3. Check both meters: Microsoft cloud storage (OneDrive plus email attachments) and Email storage (your mailbox).
  4. For each folder, permanently remove everything, or only mail older than 3, 6 or 12 months. Treat Inbox and Sent Items carefully.

5. Wait for the meter, then ask people to resend

After a big cleanup the storage warning and bars can take several hours — sometimes up to a day or more — to update. Give the meter time before you delete more mail you might still want.

When Hotmail shows you are under the limit again, tell friends and companies who wrote you while your mailbox was full to send those messages a second time. Microsoft returns bounced mail to them; nothing is held for your Live or MSN address to pick up later.

Quick check

  • From a different email address, send a one-line note to your Hotmail, Live or MSN inbox.
  • If that test arrives but an earlier message from someone else never showed up, ask that person to forward or resend it.

Questions

Is my Hotmail, Live.com or MSN address still a real mailbox?

Yes. Hotmail, Live.com and MSN addresses still work. Microsoft moved those accounts to Outlook.com, so you sign in at outlook.com with the same address and password. Your mail and storage limits are the Outlook.com limits.

How much storage does a free Hotmail account get?

Microsoft says free accounts get 15 GB of Outlook.com email storage and 5 GB of Microsoft cloud storage shared with OneDrive and email attachments. Paid Microsoft 365 subscribers get 100 GB of mailbox storage; trials do not get that extra mailbox space.

What should I empty first when Hotmail says storage is full?

Start with Deleted Items and Junk Email on your Hotmail, Live or MSN account at outlook.com; deleted mail keeps using quota until those folders are cleared with Empty folder. Next, sort by size to drop large attachments, then open Settings, then Account, then Storage to clear older mail by folder.

Does Inbox OCD delete my emails on its own?

No. Its storage cleanups move mail to Trash only after showing you the count and asking you to confirm, and you can undo them. Mail is only deleted for good when you empty Trash.
