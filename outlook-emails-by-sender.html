Outlook guide

See who sends you the most email in Outlook

Most full inboxes are full because of a few dozen senders: stores, newsletters and automatic notices. Find them, and you can clear most of your inbox in a few minutes. Here is how to find them in Outlook, and how Inbox OCD shows all of them at once.

1. Sort your inbox by sender

On Outlook.com and the new Outlook:

  1. Open your Inbox and click Filter above the message list.
  2. Choose Sort by, then From.
  3. Mail from the same sender now sits together, A to Z. Scroll down and look for long runs from one sender.

In the classic Outlook app on Windows, open the View tab and pick From under Arrangement. Mail is grouped under each sender's name.

2. Pull up one sender

  1. Type the sender's address or just their domain, like amazon.com, in the search box.
  2. Click the check box at the top of the results to select them all.
  3. Delete them, or move them to a folder.

This works well once you know who the big senders are. Sorting doesn't count them for you, so finding them is the slow part.

See every sender at once with Inbox X-Ray

Inbox X-Ray in Inbox OCD reads your recent mail and sorts every sender into groups: Real people, Newsletters, Automated & receipts, One-offs and Everyone else. It shows how many emails and senders are in each group, and how much of your inbox is noise. On one inbox, it found 184 senders and only 4 real people.

  1. On iPhone (free): get Inbox OCD from the App Store and sign in. Inbox X-Ray is the Home tab.
  2. On Mac and Windows: click X-Ray at the top of your inbox.
  3. Open a group to see its senders, with the number of emails from each one.
  4. Clear a whole group at once, or Archive, Move to Trash or Label one sender at a time.

What stays safe

  • Your mail goes directly between your device and Microsoft. It doesn't go through Inbox OCD's servers.
  • Mail moved to Trash goes to Outlook's Deleted Items, and nothing is deleted for good until that is emptied.

Questions

How do I select all emails from one sender in Outlook?

Search for the sender's address or domain, like amazon.com. Then click the check box at the top of the results to select them all, and delete or move them.

What counts as a real person in Inbox X-Ray?

Senders who write to you like a person does, not stores, newsletters or automatic notices. X-Ray sorts them for you, and you can open each group to check.
