Outlook still says full after deleting emails? Here's why

You cleared out dozens or hundreds of emails, yet Outlook or Hotmail still warns that your storage is full. Usually the mail is still waiting in a folder you haven't emptied yet, your OneDrive files and attachments have filled the cloud storage the two share, or the storage meter simply hasn't caught up. Check these five things in order.

For a full walkthrough of freeing up space across your mailbox, see Outlook storage full? Free up space safely. If Outlook already blocked send and receive, see Outlook can't send or receive because storage is full.

1. Deleted mail sits in Deleted Items and still counts

Pressing Delete does not free space right away. Mail moves to Deleted Items and keeps counting against your mailbox storage until that folder is emptied. The same goes for Junk Email.

  1. Open Deleted Items on outlook.com (this works for Hotmail, Live and MSN addresses too).
  2. Scan the list for anything you deleted by mistake and move it back to your Inbox.
  3. Click Empty folder at the top of Deleted Items.
  4. Open Junk Email, check it quickly, and click Empty folder there as well.

2. Check Recoverable Items inside Deleted Items

Even after you empty Deleted Items, Outlook.com can hold recently emptied messages in a hidden recovery area for 30 days.

  1. Open Deleted Items.
  2. At the top of the message list, click Recover items deleted from this folder.
  3. Select items you are sure you no longer need and delete them from that list so they are purged sooner.

What you can and can't clear

  • What you can clear: items shown under Recover items deleted from this folder in your personal Outlook.com mailbox.
  • What clears on its own: Anything you don't clear is removed automatically after 30 days. Work or school (Microsoft 365) accounts may keep mail longer under their organization's rules.

3. Storage is shared with OneDrive and email attachments

On Outlook.com, Microsoft tracks two separate limits: your Email storage and your Microsoft cloud storage. Email attachments count toward your Microsoft cloud storage, which is shared with OneDrive. If OneDrive files or large attachments fill that shared quota, Outlook can still warn you that storage is full even after you delete plain text emails.

  1. Sign in at outlook.com and click the gear (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Click Account, then Storage.
  3. Look at both bars: Microsoft cloud storage (OneDrive plus email attachments) and Email storage (your mailbox).
  4. If cloud storage is full, delete large attachments from your mailbox or clear large files you no longer need in OneDrive.

4. The storage number can take up to a day or more to update

Outlook.com does not always recalculate storage the moment you empty a folder. After a big cleanup, the warning banner and storage bar can take several hours — and sometimes up to a day or more — to update.

  1. Once you have emptied Deleted Items, Junk Email and any big OneDrive files, stop and wait a few hours.
  2. Sign out and sign back in, or refresh the Account > Storage page later to see the new number before you delete more mail you might want to keep.

5. Delete your biggest emails first

A thousand plain text newsletters take almost no room compared to a few emails with photos, PDFs or videos attached. If you only deleted small messages, your storage bar barely moves.

  1. Open a folder such as Inbox or Sent Items on outlook.com.
  2. Click Filter > Sort by > Size above the message list so the biggest emails appear at the top.
  3. To narrow it to attachments only, click Filter and pick Has files.
  4. Save any files you want to keep, delete the largest emails you no longer need, and empty Deleted Items again.

Questions

Why does Outlook still say my mailbox is full after I emptied Deleted Items?

Two common reasons: the storage number can take up to a day or more to update after a big cleanup, or your Microsoft cloud storage (shared with OneDrive and email attachments) is what is full. Check Settings, then Account, then Storage on outlook.com to see which limit you hit.

Can I clear Recoverable Items in Outlook.com?

Yes, in part. Open Deleted Items and click Recover items deleted from this folder to remove items listed there. Anything you don't clear is removed automatically after 30 days. Work or school (Microsoft 365) accounts may keep mail longer under their organization's rules.

How long does Outlook take to update storage after deleting emails?

It can take several hours, and sometimes up to a day or more after a large cleanup. Give it time to refresh before deleting more mail you may want to keep.

Does Inbox OCD delete my emails on its own?

No. Its storage cleanups move mail to Trash only after showing you the count and asking you to confirm, and you can undo them. Mail is only deleted for good when you empty Trash.
