How to unsubscribe from emails in Outlook and Hotmail

Unsubscribing stops the next email. It doesn't clear the ones already in your inbox, so cleaning up is two jobs. Here is how to unsubscribe in Outlook, Hotmail and on iPhone, and how to do many senders at once with Inbox OCD.

Unsubscribe or block?

  • Unsubscribe from senders you know: stores, newsletters, apps and services you signed up for.
  • Block or report as junk for spam from strangers. Don't click links in spam. It can tell the sender your address is real.

1. On Outlook.com and the new Outlook

  1. Open an email from the sender.
  2. When Outlook knows the email is from a mailing list, it shows an Unsubscribe link at the top of the email, near the sender's name. Click it and confirm.
  3. If there is no link at the top, scroll to the bottom of the email and use the sender's own unsubscribe link.

2. On iPhone

  • iPhone Mail app: an email from a mailing list shows a line at the top saying so, with an Unsubscribe link. Tap it.
  • Outlook app: scroll to the bottom of the email and tap the sender's unsubscribe link.

3. Clear what they already sent

Right-click one of their emails on Outlook.com and choose Sweep. Sweep can delete all mail from that sender, and can keep doing it for new mail. Deleted mail goes to Deleted Items first. For more ways to clear old mail, see how to delete old emails in Outlook.

Unsubscribe from many senders at once with Inbox OCD

Inbox OCD reads the unsubscribe address each sender lists in its email and sends the request for you. It shows you the list of senders with a check box first, so nothing is sent until you say so.

On iPhone (free)

  1. One sender: open an email, tap the More actions button at the top, then Unsubscribe.
  2. A whole label: touch and hold a label tab across the top of your Inbox and choose Unsubscribe from this label. Uncheck anyone you want to keep, then tap Send requests.
  3. Your noisiest senders: open Sweep from the Home tab and tap Unsubscribe on each sender you are done with.

On Mac and Windows

Each of your label rules has an Unsubscribe all button that does the same for every sender in that label.

Good to know

  • After the requests go out, Inbox OCD hides those senders' mail in your inbox. You can undo the hiding right after.
  • Requests can't be taken back once they reach the sender.
  • Some senders only offer a web page to unsubscribe. For those, Inbox OCD shows an Open unsubscribe page button.

Questions

Is it safe to click Unsubscribe?

For senders you know, like stores and newsletters you signed up for, yes. For spam from strangers, don't. Use Report junk or Block instead, because clicking links in spam can confirm your address is active.

Why do I still get emails after unsubscribing?

Senders can take a few days to remove you. In the US, the CAN-SPAM Act gives them up to 10 business days. If mail keeps coming after that, block the sender.

Can Inbox OCD take back an unsubscribe?

No. Once a request reaches the sender, it can't be taken back. Inbox OCD also hides that sender's mail in your inbox, and that part can be undone.

Does this work for Hotmail and Live addresses?

Yes. Hotmail, Live and MSN addresses are Outlook.com accounts, so they use the same Outlook.com steps.
