Your mail stays yours

It runs on your computer

Mail goes straight between your computer and Microsoft or Google. Your rules and sign-in are stored on your device. Details are in our privacy policy.

No AI reads your mail

Inbox OCD does not send your email to any AI service. Your messages stay between your device and your mail provider.

Nothing changes until you say so

Filing, trashing and snoozing show in the app first; your real mailbox isn't touched unless you turn that on. What it files can be undone. Deleting a calendar event asks first and can't be undone inside Inbox OCD.

Outlook and Gmail together

With the Standard license, both show in one inbox, and one set of rules covers both. Gmail and Outlook don't share rules with each other; Inbox OCD gives you one set.

How Inbox OCD uses your Google and Microsoft data

Inbox OCD is a desktop email client. With your permission, it connects to your Gmail account (read, label, and send mail; read your calendar) and your Outlook account (read and send mail) to display your messages, file them according to rules you create, and send email you compose. Desktop mail sync is direct to your device. Google access also supports filter import, Other Contacts email matching and confirmed calendar-event deletion. Inbox OCD does not send message content to any AI service.

Inbox OCD's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details in our privacy policy.
