What Inbox OCD does

  • Inbox X-Ray counts who is writing to you: newsletters, receipts, one-offs, and real people.
  • One button files the newsletters and receipts out of your way. Nothing is deleted, and you can undo it.
  • New senders wait at the door until you say yes or no.
  • Storage cleanup finds your biggest emails and the senders taking up the most space, and clears them together. See how to free up Outlook storage.
  • Senders: for anyone who emails you, choose to file to a label, mute, send to Trash, or mark as VIP. See how to delete old emails in Outlook.
  • Undo: every batch it files is written down in a list, and you can take any of it back.

Where your mail goes

Inbox OCD runs on your computer. Your mail goes straight between your computer and Microsoft or Google, your rules and sign-in are stored on your device, and no AI reads your mail. Details are in our privacy policy.

Whatever cleaner you choose, it's worth reading its privacy policy to see where your mail is handled.

When a subscription cleaner may suit you better

  • You don't use a Mac or Windows computer. Inbox OCD needs one: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 11 or later, or Windows 10 or 11.
  • You want a tool that works only in a web browser, with nothing to install.
