What Inbox OCD does

Works with Outlook (Outlook license, and in the free trial)

  • Inbox X-Ray counts who is writing to you: newsletters, receipts, one-offs, and real people.
  • One button files the newsletters and receipts out of your way in Inbox OCD. Nothing is deleted, and you can undo it.
  • Screener: turn it on in Settings, and new senders wait at the door until you say yes or no.
  • Storage rescue finds your biggest emails and the senders taking up the most space, and clears them together. See how to free up Outlook storage.
  • Senders: for anyone who emails you, choose to file to a label, mute, send to Trash, or mark as VIP. See how to delete old emails in Outlook.
  • Watching for: say who an email you're waiting for will come from, or a few words it might have. When it arrives, it goes to the top of your inbox.
  • Accounts vault: keep your passwords next to the email you used for them, only on your computer, locked with Touch ID or Windows Hello and a master password. A copied password clears from the clipboard in 30 seconds.
  • Undo: every batch it files is written down in a list, and you can take any of it back.

Gmail only (Standard: Gmail + Outlook in one inbox)

  • Gmail + Outlook in one inbox, with one set of rules for both.
  • Clean up mail by sender, words and Older than, all at once.

Google is still reviewing Inbox OCD, so only a limited number of Gmail accounts can connect for now.

Where your mail goes

Inbox OCD runs on your computer. Your mail goes straight between your computer and Microsoft or Google, and your rules and sign-in are stored on your device. Details are in our privacy policy.

Whatever cleaner you choose, it's worth reading its privacy policy to see where your mail is handled.

When a subscription cleaner may suit you better

  • You don't use a Mac or Windows computer. Inbox OCD needs one: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 11 or later, or Windows 10 or 11.
  • You want a tool that works only in a web browser, with nothing to install.
