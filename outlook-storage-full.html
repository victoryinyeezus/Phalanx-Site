Outlook storage full? Free up space safely

When Outlook says your mailbox is full, new mail can stop arriving and you may not be able to send. The fix is to find what is taking the space, move it out, and then empty Deleted Items. Here is how to do it in Outlook itself, and how to do the same job in bulk with Inbox OCD. If send and receive already stopped, start with Outlook can't send or receive because storage is full. Using an old Hotmail, Live or MSN address? See Hotmail full? How to free up space.

Before you delete anything

  • Save what you need first. Download attachments you want to keep, like photos, tax papers and receipts.
  • Deleting is two steps. Mail you delete goes to Deleted Items first. It keeps using space until Deleted Items is emptied.
  • Emptying Deleted Items is final. Check what is in there before you empty it.

1. See what is using the space

On Outlook.com (this includes Hotmail, Live and MSN addresses):

  1. Sign in at outlook.com and click the gear (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Open Account, then Storage.
  3. You'll see how much space your mail uses, and a list of folders with the size of each one.

On Outlook.com, attachments also count toward your Microsoft cloud storage, which is shared with OneDrive. If OneDrive is full, cleaning up mail attachments helps there too.

2. Find the biggest emails

A few large emails often take more room than thousands of small ones.

  • Outlook.com: open the folder, then click Filter > Sort by > Size above the message list. The biggest emails come first.
  • Only emails with attachments (Outlook.com): click Filter and pick Has files.

In the classic Outlook app on Windows (not Outlook.com):

  • Go to File → Tools → Mailbox Cleanup. It can find items larger than a size you choose and show the size of each folder.

Open a few to check, then select the ones you don't need and delete them.

3. Clear out old mail by folder

On the Outlook.com Storage page from step 1, each folder has options to delete everything in it, or only messages older than 3, 6 or 12 months. This is a quick way to clear an old newsletters folder. Be careful with your Inbox and Sent Items.

4. Clear senders who fill the inbox

Right-click an email from a sender who writes too often and choose Sweep. Sweep can move or delete all mail from that sender, and can keep doing it for new mail. Mail that Sweep deletes still goes to Deleted Items first.

5. Empty Deleted Items and Junk

  1. Open Deleted Items. Look through it for anything you still want and move it back.
  2. Click Empty folder. Do the same for Junk Email.

Outlook.com also empties Deleted Items by itself after about 30 days, but you get the space back sooner if you do it now. The storage number can take a while to update after a big cleanup, so give it time before you clean more. If it still says full afterward, see why Outlook still says full after deleting emails.

Questions

Why is my Outlook storage still full after I deleted emails?

Deleted emails go to Deleted Items and still use space there. Empty Deleted Items to get the space back. The storage number can also take a while to update.

Do attachments count toward Outlook storage?

Yes. On Outlook.com, attachments also count toward your Microsoft cloud storage, which is shared with OneDrive. Deleting big emails with attachments frees that space.

Does Inbox OCD delete anything on its own?

No. Its storage cleanups move mail to Trash only after you confirm, and you can undo them. Mail is only deleted for good when you empty Trash or choose Delete forever.

Does Inbox OCD work with Hotmail and Live addresses?

Yes. Hotmail, Live and MSN addresses are Outlook.com accounts, and Inbox OCD works with Outlook and Microsoft 365.
