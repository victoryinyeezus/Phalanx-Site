How to clean up your Outlook inbox on iPhone

Clearing an Outlook inbox one email at a time on a phone takes forever. The Outlook and Mail apps can clear a screenful at a time. To clear whole senders at once, like every newsletter and store email, use the free Inbox OCD app. Here is how to do each.

Before you start

  • Archive is not delete. Archived mail leaves your inbox but still uses storage.
  • Delete is two steps. Deleted mail goes to Deleted Items first. It keeps using space until Deleted Items is emptied.
  • Save what you need first. Keep photos, receipts and papers you still want.

1. In the Outlook app

  1. Open your Inbox and touch and hold an email until a check mark appears.
  2. Tap more emails to add them.
  3. Tap Delete or Archive at the bottom.

To go faster, set your swipes: open Outlook's Settings, then Swipe options, and set a swipe to Delete or Archive. Then one swipe clears one email.

2. In the iPhone Mail app

If your Outlook or Hotmail account is added to the iPhone's own Mail app:

  1. Open the Inbox, tap the More button (three dots) at the top, then tap Select. On older iPhone software, tap Edit instead.
  2. Tap the emails you want. In List View you can also tap Select All.
  3. Tap Trash. If you see Archive instead, touch and hold it to pick Trash.

Be careful with Select All. It picks everything in the Inbox, including mail from real people.

3. Stop the mail that keeps coming

Clearing old mail doesn't stop new mail. In the Mail app, an email from a mailing list shows a line at the top saying it is from a mailing list, with an Unsubscribe link. In any app, you can also scroll to the bottom of the email and use the sender's own unsubscribe link. More in how to unsubscribe from emails in Outlook.

Clear whole senders at once with Inbox OCD

Inbox OCD for iPhone is free on the App Store. It shows who is filling your inbox and lets you clear them in groups instead of one email at a time.

  1. Get Inbox OCD from the App Store and sign in with your Outlook, Hotmail or Microsoft 365 account.
  2. The Home tab opens with Inbox X-Ray. It reads your newest mail on your iPhone and shows how much of it is Real people, Newsletters, Automated & receipts and One-offs.
  3. Tap a group to see its senders. Tap Archive all to clear the whole group out of your inbox, or use a sender's own menu to Archive, Move to Trash or Label just that sender.
  4. For a faster pass, tap Sweep at the top of Home. It shows your noisiest senders one at a time: tap Unsubscribe, Trash, Keep or Skip. It takes about a minute.
  5. If your mailbox is full, open Storage rescue from Home. It lists your biggest emails and biggest senders, and can Clean up the top 20 or Trash everything over 10 MB.
  6. Mail moved to Trash goes to Outlook's Deleted Items. Empty Deleted Items to get the space back.

What stays safe

  • Inbox OCD reads your mail on your iPhone. Your mail goes directly between your iPhone and Microsoft.
  • Trash means Outlook's Deleted Items. Nothing is deleted for good until Deleted Items is emptied.
  • Unsubscribe sends a real request to the sender. It lists the senders with a check box first, and a request can't be taken back once it is sent.

Questions

Does archiving emails free up Outlook storage?

No. Archive moves mail out of your inbox, but it stays in your mailbox and still uses space. To free space, move mail to Deleted Items and then empty Deleted Items.

Is Inbox OCD free on iPhone?

Yes. The iPhone app is free on the App Store, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does Inbox OCD work with Hotmail on iPhone?

Yes. Hotmail, Live and MSN addresses are Outlook.com accounts, and the iPhone app works with Outlook, Hotmail and Microsoft 365.

Does Inbox OCD send my mail to its own servers?

No. The iPhone app reads your mail on the phone. Your mail goes directly between your iPhone and Microsoft.
