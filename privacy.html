INBOX OCD

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Inbox OCD connects your Gmail and Microsoft Outlook accounts so you can read and organize mail. In the desktop app, ordinary mailbox sync runs directly between your device and Google or Microsoft. Optional AI features can send message content to an AI provider, and prepaid AI credits route that content through our service. These data flows and your controls are described below.

What Inbox OCD accesses

When you connect an account, you grant Inbox OCD permission through Google's or Microsoft's official sign-in (OAuth). Depending on the permissions you approve, Inbox OCD accesses:

  • Email messages (Gmail and/or Outlook): read messages to display your inbox; apply labels/folders and read state according to rules you create; send messages you compose, reply to, or schedule.
  • Mailbox settings: read your existing Gmail filters and labels, and Outlook rules and categories, so Inbox OCD can import and display them.
  • Calendar (Google): read events on your primary Google Calendar to display them in Inbox OCD's calendar view, and delete an event when you explicitly choose to. Deletion is always initiated by you, is confirmed first, and removes the event from your Google Calendar everywhere — Inbox OCD cannot undo it. Inbox OCD does not create or edit calendar events.
  • Other Contacts (Google): read email addresses automatically saved by Google and match them on your device against message senders in Inbox X-Ray. This helps classify known correspondents. Contact names are not requested; displayed sender names come from message headers. These fetched contact addresses are not uploaded to our service, and Inbox OCD does not modify contacts.
  • Basic profile (email address, name): to label which account is which inside the app.

Where your data lives

  • Your email is fetched directly from Google and Microsoft to your device over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • In the desktop app, sign-in tokens, sender rules, settings, and the action journal are stored locally on your device (on macOS under ~/Library/Application Support/Gridline; on Windows under %APPDATA%\Gridline).
  • Ordinary desktop mailbox sync does not pass through our service. Our Cloudflare service handles account authentication, AI credits and AI requests. In credits mode, relevant message content passes through it to Anthropic; our application does not persist that content.
  • For clients that use our hosted mail-authorization flow, the service exchanges and refreshes authorization tokens with Google or Microsoft and stores access and refresh tokens encrypted, together with account identifiers, granted scopes and expiry times. This differs from desktop-local authorization storage.

Optional AI features

AI requires you to configure an AI mode. Once AI is configured, automatic message summaries are enabled by default: opening a message can send its content for summarization without a separate Summarize click. You can disable automatic summaries using the automatic-summary control. On-demand summaries and assistant requests remain available when you choose to use them. There are two request paths:

  • Your own API key. You add a key for an AI provider of your choice (e.g., Anthropic, OpenAI, or OpenRouter). The relevant message content is sent from your device directly to that provider, under your account and their privacy terms. Your key is stored locally on your device. No request touches us.
  • Prepaid credits. If you buy credits instead of supplying a key, the request is sent to our Cloudflare Worker, which forwards it to Anthropic using our key and meters what you spent. The message content you submit passes through that Worker in transit. It is not written to disk, not logged, and not retained — we record only the model used, the request status, and token counts for billing. Our records contain no column for message content. If you would rather no request of yours ever transit our infrastructure, use your own API key instead.

In both modes, the relevant content is sent for the requested feature, including automatic summaries when you open a message with that setting enabled. Disabling automatic summaries stops that automatic trigger; manual AI requests still send the relevant content. AI providers process requests under their own applicable terms and retention policies. Removing local data does not delete data already processed by those providers.

Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

Inbox OCD's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: Google user data is used only to provide the email, calendar, known-contact matching and configured AI features described here; it is not used for advertising; it is not sold; it is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide features you choose to use (including automatic summaries after AI is configured, unless you disable them) or as required by law; and humans do not read it.

Payments

If you buy AI credits, the payment is processed by Stripe, and you enter your card details on Stripe's own checkout page. We never see or store your card number. We keep a record of the purchase (an account identifier, the amount, and the resulting credit balance) so credits can be applied and reconciled. Stripe's handling of your payment data is governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

What we collect about you

Our service processes account identifiers and authentication information, purchase and usage records for credits, and operational information needed to secure and run the service. If you email support, we receive what you send. If you use the website early-access form, your email address is sent through FormSubmit to our support inbox to handle your request.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your personal data or mailbox contents. We share data as needed for the features described here: Google and Microsoft for connected accounts; your chosen AI provider for BYOK; Cloudflare and Anthropic for prepaid AI requests; Stripe for payments; and FormSubmit for website early-access requests. AI transfers include automatic summaries when enabled. We may also disclose data when required by law.

Data retention and deletion

Disconnecting a desktop account removes its locally stored authorization. Uninstalling the desktop app and deleting its application-data folder removes local app data, but does not erase provider-side mail or records held by our service. You can revoke access from your Google Account permissions or Microsoft account permissions page. Revocation stops use of the revoked authorization; it does not itself erase stored records.

For hosted account, authorization-token, credit or early-access records, contact support@inboxocd.com to request deletion. Payment records may need to be retained for accounting, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Local disconnect should not be assumed to delete hosted records. Our AI request handler does not store message content; provider-side retention is governed by the provider's terms.

Security

All communication with Google and Microsoft uses HTTPS. Sign-in uses each provider's official OAuth flow in your own browser — Inbox OCD never sees or stores your password. Remote images in email are blocked by default to prevent tracking pixels.

Children

Inbox OCD is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the date above.
