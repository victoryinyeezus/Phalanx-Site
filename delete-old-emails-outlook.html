Outlook guide

How to delete old emails in Outlook and Hotmail

Years of old mail make Outlook slow to search and can fill your mailbox. You can clear it by date or by sender. The safe way is the same every time: pick the mail, look at what you picked, move it to Deleted Items, and only then empty Deleted Items.

Hotmail, Live and MSN addresses are Outlook.com accounts, so the steps for Outlook.com work for them too.

Delete emails older than a date

All the old mail in a folder

  1. On outlook.com, click the gear (Settings), then Account, then Storage.
  2. Find the folder you want to clear, such as Inbox or a newsletters folder.
  3. Choose to delete messages older than 3, 6 or 12 months, or everything in that folder.

Old mail you want to look at first

  1. Open the folder and click Filter > Sort by > Date above the message list, then switch the order so the oldest mail is at the top.
  2. Click the first email you want to remove, hold Shift, and click the last one. Everything between them is selected.
  3. Scroll through the selection, then press Delete.

In the classic Outlook app on Windows (not Outlook.com), File → Tools → Mailbox Cleanup can also find items older than a number of days.

Delete every email from one sender

This is the fastest way to clear shops, newsletters and alerts.

  1. Type the sender's address, or just their domain like amazon.com, into the Outlook search box and press Enter.
  2. Look over the results. Shops send receipts too, and you may want to keep those.
  3. Select the ones to remove and press Delete.

Or right-click one of their emails and choose Sweep. Sweep can delete all mail from that sender, and can keep doing it for new mail they send.

Before you delete for good

  • Deleted Items is your safety net. Deleted mail waits there, and you can move it back. Outlook.com empties it by itself after about 30 days.
  • Emptying Deleted Items is final. Outlook.com sometimes lets you recover mail for a short time after, with Recover items deleted from this folder, but don't count on it.
  • Shift+Delete in classic Outlook skips Deleted Items. Use plain Delete unless you're sure.
  • Work accounts may differ. Your company may keep or remove mail on its own schedule.

Questions

Can I get deleted Outlook emails back?

Yes, while they are in Deleted Items. Open Deleted Items and move them back. Once Deleted Items is emptied, you may only be able to recover them for a short time, so check before you empty it.

How do I delete all emails from one sender in Outlook?

Search for the sender's address or domain, select the results and press Delete. Or right-click one of their emails and use Sweep.

Are the steps the same for Hotmail?

Yes. Hotmail, Live and MSN addresses are Outlook.com accounts, so they use the same Outlook.com steps.

Can Inbox OCD delete Outlook emails by date?

Not in one step. Use Outlook's own date tools for that. Inbox OCD clears Outlook mail by sender and by size, and its Older than tool works with Gmail.
