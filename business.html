Inbox OCD

Fast email sorting for the whole office, without sending your email anywhere.

Inbox OCD runs on each person's own Mac or Windows computer. Set up label rules once and it keeps sorting new mail into the right place, so people spend their day on the work, not the inbox.

Fast where it counts

Inbox OCD decides where 10,000 emails belong in about 1/40 of a second using 20 rules.1 Then it works through the changes in the background while you keep working, with a progress bar, a Stop button and Undo for every batch.

Time back every day

An example, not a promise: if filing email takes someone 10 minutes a workday, that's about 40 hours a year. For a team of 10, about 400 hours. Label rules keep doing that filing for you as new mail arrives.

Works with what you already use

Gmail and Microsoft 365 / Outlook, in one inbox. No new email system, no migration and nothing to install on a server.

Security in plain terms

  • Only your people have their logins. Everyone signs in on Google's or Microsoft's own page. Inbox OCD never sees passwords.
  • Mail access stays on each computer. The sign-in token is locked with the Mac Keychain or Windows' built-in encryption.
  • The desktop app never sends email or logins to our servers, and no AI reads your mail.
  • Access can be removed in one click from Google or Microsoft security settings.

Not available yet: SOC 2 reports, single sign-on and a central admin console. We'll tell you plainly what we can and can't do for your security review.

On phones: a companion app for mobile is coming soon.
